# Craving — The Engine of the Habit Loop

## Overview
In *The Power of Habit*, **craving** is described as the strong anticipation or desire for a reward that drives habit formation and repetition.

## Key Points
- Craving is what makes a cue trigger a routine repeatedly.
- It is the anticipation of the **reward**, not just the reward itself, that motivates behavior.
- Cravings create a neurological craving loop involving dopamine and reward pathways.
- Strong cravings lead to persistent habits that are difficult to break.

## Role in Memory and Habit Formation
- Cravings link **emotional centers** (like the amygdala) with **reward anticipation systems** in the brain.
- The brain learns to associate cues with the expected reward, reinforcing the habit loop.
- Craving strengthens **procedural memories** by motivating repeated behaviors.
