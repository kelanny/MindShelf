# Keystone Habit — The Habits That Trigger Widespread Change

## Overview
A **keystone habit** is a powerful habit that, when changed, influences many other habits and behaviors, creating a ripple effect across life.

## Key Points
- Keystone habits shape identity and create frameworks for additional behavior changes.
- They often affect multiple areas (e.g., exercise improving diet and productivity).
- Changing a keystone habit can reorganize other neural pathways and habits.
- They foster belief in change and provide momentum for self-improvement.

## Role in Habit Architecture
- Keystone habits influence **executive control** and self-regulation.
- They engage **identity-based memory**, strengthening "I am the kind of person who..." beliefs.
- Lead to more sustainable, long-term habit changes through positive feedback loops.

## Related Brain Areas
- **[Prefrontal Cortex](prefrontal_cortex.md)**: Maintains goal-directed behavior and executive function.
- **[Anterior Cingulate Cortex](anterior_cingulate_cortex.md)**: Monitors conflicts and shifts attention during change.
- **[Reward Systems](./reward_systems)**: Reinforce positive behavior through dopamine feedback.
