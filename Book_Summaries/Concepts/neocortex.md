# Neocortex — The Seat of Higher-Order Cognition and Memory Integration

## Overview
The **Neocortex** is the largest part of the cerebral cortex and is responsible for advanced brain functions such as sensory perception, reasoning, and conscious thought. It plays a critical role in **declarative memory** and **memory consolidation**.

## Key Functions
- Processes sensory input and integrates information from multiple brain areas.
- Supports **conscious, explicit memory** formation including facts and events.
- Enables complex reasoning, planning, and decision-making.
- Involved in **long-term storage** of memories after hippocampal consolidation.

## Role in Memory Formation
- Receives consolidated memories from the **hippocampus** for long-term storage.
- Supports **semantic memory** (facts, concepts) and **episodic memory** (personal experiences).
- Facilitates **working memory** and executive functions critical to planning and behavior regulation.
- Enables flexible use of memory in novel situations.

## Related Brain Areas
- **[Hippocampus](./hippocampus)**: Initial encoding and consolidation of declarative memories.
- **[Prefrontal Cortex](./prefrontal_cortex)**: Executive control, decision-making, and working memory.
- **[Sensory Cortices](./)**: Process modality-specific information for memory encoding.

> 🧠 The neocortex allows memories to be consciously accessed, manipulated, and used to guide future behavior.
