# Prefrontal Cortex — The Executive Memory Controller

## Overview
The **Prefrontal Cortex (PFC)** is located in the frontal lobe and is essential for **working memory**, **decision-making**, and **retrieval of memories**.

## Key Functions
- **Working Memory**: Temporarily holds and manipulates information — like the brain’s “notepad.”
- **Memory Retrieval**: Helps recall stored declarative memories by coordinating with the hippocampus and other brain regions.
- **Planning and Organization**: Critical for goal-directed behavior based on past memories.
- **Attention Regulation**: Filters relevant information to improve memory encoding and retrieval.

## Role in Memory Formation
- Collaborates with the hippocampus to **retrieve conscious memories**.
- Involved in **strategic encoding** — deciding what information to remember.
- Supports **temporal organization** of memories (remembering sequences and timing of events).

## Related Brain Areas
- **Hippocampus**: Works closely to encode and retrieve explicit memories.
- **Parietal Cortex**: Assists in attention and spatial memory.
- **Basal Ganglia**: Involved in habit formation and decision-making loops.
