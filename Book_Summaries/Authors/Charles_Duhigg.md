# 👤 Charles Duhigg

## 📝 Bio
Pulitzer Prize-winning journalist and author known for his work exploring the science of habit, productivity, and performance. Former reporter for *The New York Times*.

---

## 📚 Notable Works

- **The Power of Habit** (2012)
- **Smarter Faster Better** (2016)
