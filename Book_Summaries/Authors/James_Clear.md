## 🎓 Background

James Clear is an American author and speaker known for his expertise in habits, decision-making, and continuous improvement. He holds a degree in biomechanics from Denison University and has a strong interest in how human behavior can be shaped by small, incremental changes.

---

## 📘 Notable Work

### Atomic Habits (2018)

James Clear is best known for *Atomic Habits*, a bestselling book that explores the science of habit formation. The book has sold millions of copies worldwide and has been translated into more than 50 languages.

> “You do not rise to the level of your goals. You fall to the level of your systems.”  
> — *Atomic Habits*, James Clear

The book is built around the concept that small changes—if practiced consistently—lead to remarkable results. It emphasizes systems over goals and explains how habits shape identity.

---

## 🧠 Core Themes of His Work

- **Behavioral change through systems**
- **The role of identity in forming lasting habits**
- **The power of small, consistent improvements**
- **Environmental design as a behavior cue**
- **Feedback loops and habit stacking**
