# Book Info: *Atomic Habits*

**Title:** Atomic Habits  
**Subtitle:** An Easy & Proven Way to Build Good Habits & Break Bad Ones  
**Author:** James Clear  
**Publisher:** Avery (an imprint of Penguin Random House)  
**Publication Date:** October 16, 2018  
**Language:** English  
**Pages:** 320  
**ISBN-13:** 978-0735211292  
**Genres:** Self-help, Psychology, Personal Development, Productivity  
**Audience:** General readers interested in personal growth, productivity, habit formation, and behavioral psychology.

---

## 📚 Overview

*Atomic Habits* is a comprehensive guide on how to change habits and improve behaviors using a proven system grounded in behavioral science. The book demonstrates how making small changes can lead to remarkable results over time. Rather than focusing solely on goals, Clear emphasizes the power of systems, identity, and environment in shaping long-term habits.

---

## 🔬 Scientific Foundations

James Clear draws from:

- Behavioral psychology (e.g., B.F. Skinner, Charles Duhigg)
- Neuroscience of habit loops
- Cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT)
- Real-world case studies (sports, business, health)

---

## 🧱 Structure of the Book

The book is divided into an introduction and four main parts, each centered around a "law" of behavior change:

1. **The 1st Law – Make It Obvious**
2. **The 2nd Law – Make It Attractive**
3. **The 3rd Law – Make It Easy**
4. **The 4th Law – Make It Satisfying**

Each section is filled with principles, stories, and actionable advice that support building better habits and breaking bad ones.
