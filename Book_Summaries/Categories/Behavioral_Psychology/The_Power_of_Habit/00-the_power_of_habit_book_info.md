# 📘 Book Information

## Title
The Power of Habit: Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business

## Author
See full profile: [Charles Duhigg](Charles_Duhigg.md)

## Year Published
2012

## ISBN
978-1400069286

## Summary
A deep dive into how habits are formed, how they shape behavior, and how they can be changed—individually, organizationally, and societally. The book draws on neuroscience, psychology, and case studies.
