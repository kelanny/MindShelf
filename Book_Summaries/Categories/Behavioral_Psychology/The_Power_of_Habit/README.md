# 📘 The Power of Habit  
**Why We Do What We Do in Life and Business**  
*by Charles Duhigg*

---

## 📚 About the Book

> _“Change might not be fast and it isn’t always easy. But with time and effort, almost any habit can be reshaped.”_

**The Power of Habit** explores the science behind how habits work, how they’re formed, and how they can be changed. Duhigg presents a framework—the **habit loop (cue → routine → reward)**—and illustrates how individuals, organizations, and societies can harness it for transformation.

## 🧠 Core Themes

- The Habit Loop: **Cue → Routine → Reward**
- **Craving** is what powers the loop
- **Keystone habits** drive widespread change
- Habits operate in the background until reshaped
- Change comes by keeping the cue and reward, but altering the routine
- Organizations and societies are built on collective habits
- **Awareness** and **belief** are critical to transformation
